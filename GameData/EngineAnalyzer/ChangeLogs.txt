V2.2.0.1 (stock fork: github.com/Void-Seeker/KSP-EngineAnalyzer, branch stock)
1.无需真实燃料（RealFuels）也能正常使用；安装了RealFuels时行为与原版相同。
2.燃料筛选：按发动机实际使用的推进剂分类（液体燃料、氧化剂、单组元等），按使用数量排序。
3.燃料质量与容积按升计算：原版单位（液体燃料/氧化剂/单组元/固体燃料每单位5升，氙气0.1升）不再导致质量偏差5倍；离子发动机不再把电力算入混合密度。
4.同步VAB数据/锁定当前分级：从原版油箱读取容积（不计指令舱的单组元）。
5.同步全重不再包含当前分级油箱中的推进剂（原版油箱在VAB中默认装满，原先被重复计算，Δv偏低）。
6.源代码可编译：删除重复声明的mousePos；增加build.sh与AssemblyInfo.cs。
1.Works without Real Fuels; with Real Fuels installed it behaves as upstream.
2.Fuel filter: one category per propellant the engines burn (Liquid Fuel, Oxidizer, Monopropellant, ...), most used first.
3.Fuel mass and volume per liter: stock units (5 L for LiquidFuel/Oxidizer/MonoPropellant/SolidFuel, 0.1 L for XenonGas) no longer put fuel mass 5x off; ion engines no longer count Electric Charge in the mix density.
4.Sync VAB Data / Lock Current Stage read the volume of stock tanks (a command pod's monopropellant isn't counted).
5.Sync Mass leaves out the propellant in the current stage's tanks (stock tanks come full in the VAB, so it counted twice and delta-v came out too low).
6.The source builds: duplicate mousePos declaration removed; build.sh and AssemblyInfo.cs added.
